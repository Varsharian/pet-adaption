Pet Care Tips 🐾

Simple tips to keep your furry friend healthy and happy.

🍖 Healthy Food

Feed your dog a balanced diet with fresh water every day. Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, and junk food.

🏃 Daily Exercise

Take your dog for at least 30 minutes of walking or playtime daily. Exercise keeps them active and healthy.

🛁 Grooming

Brush your dog's fur regularly and bathe them when necessary. Keep their nails trimmed.

💉 Vaccination

Follow your veterinarian's vaccination schedule and regular health checkups.

❤️ Love & Care

Spend quality time with your dog every day. Love, patience, and attention improve their emotional well-being.

👨‍⚕️ Veterinary Visits

Visit a veterinarian every 6–12 months for routine checkups and preventive care.

Emergency Tips 🚑

  • Always keep fresh drinking water available.
  • Keep harmful medicines away from pets.
  • Save your nearest veterinarian's phone number.
  • Keep your dog indoors during extreme weather.
  • Ensure your dog wears an ID tag.
